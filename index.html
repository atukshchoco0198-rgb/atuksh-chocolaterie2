Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our freshly crafted chocolates.

Unlike commercial chocolates that sit on shelves for months with chemical stabilizers, we melt, temper, fill, and pack your chocolates only after receiving your specific order. This delivers unparalleled freshness, velvet texture, and intense cocoa flavor.
Yes, absolutely! All our chocolates, bonbons, barks, and fillings are 100% vegetarian and eggless, prepared in our hygienic home studio.
Yes! Atuksh Chocolaterie delivers freshly handcrafted chocolates and custom gift hampers across Panchkula, Chandigarh, Mohali, and the entire Tricity region. We also ship to select cities pan-India for special occasions. Contact us on WhatsApp to check delivery availability for your location.
Atuksh Chocolaterie is a home-based artisanal chocolate brand in Panchkula, Haryana, India — right in the heart of the Chandigarh Tricity area. Founded by Atul and Diksha, every chocolate is handcrafted fresh from our home studio with Pahadi-inspired love. Reach us anytime on WhatsApp to place an order!
Because everything is handcrafted fresh, we recommend ordering 1-2 days in advance for regular boxes, and 3-5 days in advance for large celebration hampers or bulk orders. If you need urgent same-day treats, message us on WhatsApp and we will check our active tempering batch!
Our chocolates are best enjoyed within 3 to 4 weeks of delivery. Store them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight (16°C – 20°C). In warmer months, store them in the refrigerator inside their airtight box and bring to room temperature for 10 minutes before tasting.
